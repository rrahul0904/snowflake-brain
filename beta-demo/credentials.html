Verified credentials

Prove your SnowPro credential with issuer-backed evidence.

This public demo verifies a candidate's public Credly badge instead of trusting a screenshot or self-declared certification field. The production implementation on main adds authenticated candidate ownership, persistence, duplicate-claim protection, immutable verification events, and automatic visibility controls.
