# Functional Beta Learning Routes

The public beta routes are intentionally interactive rather than marketing placeholders.

- `#guide` — five weighted domains, nineteen task statements, lesson detail panels, exam cues, domain practice actions.
- `#practice` — twenty original demo questions with immediate grading/explanations, score, navigator and restart.
- `#mock` — ten-question timed beta mock with hidden answers until submission, navigation, score, answer review and domain breakdown.
- `#adaptive` — recommendations calculated from local practice and submitted mock evidence, adjusted by COF-C03 domain weights.
- `#progress` — session-level practice progress already present in the beta shell; adaptive/mock state persists locally for continuity.
- `#insights` — clickable full-note insight cards with related-practice actions.
- `#membership` — current five-plan product packaging for feedback; checkout disabled in beta.

The beta remains an independent preparation resource and does not present demo questions or readiness scores as official Snowflake exam content/scoring.
