# Publishing

The 2026-10-08 publishing receipt records the exact validated corpus used to produce the certification Word/PDF package.

Current published scope:

- 12 tracked Snowflake certification banks
- 5,000 questions per certification
- 5,000 paired solutions per certification
- 60,000 questions + 60,000 solutions
- Word and PDF certification books for every tracked exam
- Word and PDF master index
- advanced QA and publishing completeness checks

Large derived Word/PDF binaries are distributed as release artifacts/downloadable deliverables rather than committed to Git. The canonical question-bank source remains reproducible from the curated records, generator catalog, materializer, and CI validation workflow.

Automated QA passing does not mean every generated record has been individually human-reviewed. Generated records retain `draft` status until explicitly promoted after source and ambiguity review.
