# Content Integrity, Copyright, and Certification-Integrity Policy

**Status:** active V26 product policy  
**Scope:** lessons, practice questions, mock exams, explanations, reference material, journal content, build exercises, UI copy, generated drafts, private corpora, and contributor material.

This is an operational product standard, not legal advice or a claim that any copyright or trademark registration has been filed or granted.

## Independent preparation only

Snowflake Certification Guide publishes independently authored certification-preparation material. We do not knowingly copy, buy, solicit, import, reconstruct, publish, or train from live, leaked, stolen, recalled, or confidential certification exam questions or answer keys. Exam dumps, braindumps, "actual exam" repositories, and unlicensed commercial question-bank/course wording are prohibited sources.

Public technical facts may be researched from lawful sources. Snowflake documentation and official SnowPro certification/program pages are preferred factual authorities. A public source may establish a fact; it does not grant permission to copy protected expression.

## Source provenance

Material that makes a significant technical or certification-policy claim should be able to answer:

1. Where did the fact come from?
2. When was it last verified?
3. What happens if the source changes?

Source freshness may flag content for review. It does not silently rewrite a governed question, answer, explanation, or historical exam sitting.

If a public certification fact cannot be verified from the authoritative source used for the release, the product stores it as unknown/not verified and omits it from the public UI rather than guessing from a third-party preparation site.

## Question integrity

A production question must:

- map to a public certification objective or documented learning objective;
- use independently authored stem, options, rationale, and distractor logic;
- avoid claims that it is a real or recalled SnowPro exam item;
- preserve source-reference metadata where supported;
- pass structural QA and the governed editorial lifecycle;
- remain private until its approved release and entitlement permit delivery;
- never reveal correct answers or explanations in an active timed sitting before grading.

Automated QA is not human SME approval. The governed release path keeps structural validation, editorial review, SME approval, staging, activation, and rollback as distinct states.

## AI-assisted authoring

AI may assist with drafting, classification, variation, or QA only when inputs are lawful. AI output remains a draft until the applicable editorial gate is satisfied. Do not prompt a model with substantial unlicensed third-party course/question material to create a substitute or derivative version.

AI assistance does not replace provenance, factual review, originality review, security controls, or human governance.

## Build exercises and instructional coaching

The product prefers deterministic, authored learning aids for build exercises: scenario, steps, starter material, progressive hints, validation checks, expected outcome, and solution/explanation. An open-ended AI tutor is not required to reproduce another product's interaction pattern and must not be added without separate cost, security, hallucination, prompt-governance, abuse, and observability design.

The service must not imply that Snowflake Inc. operates, sponsors, approves, certifies, or endorses this independent preparation product. Do not use Snowflake or SnowPro logos as the product's own brand without documented permission. Any future trademark filing should concern a distinct, cleared platform brand rather than a third-party mark.

## Copyright and contributor rights

Before publishing commissioned or contributed material, obtain appropriate authorship/ownership or license documentation. Payment alone must not be treated as proof that copyright transferred. Contributors must not provide confidential exam material or material they lack the right to provide.

Do not represent the platform, curriculum, source code, or private corpus as copyright-registered or trademark-registered unless the applicable filing/registration actually exists.

## Corrections and complaints

A credible factual, copyright, provenance, or certification-integrity report should be logged and reviewed. The relevant item may be quarantined while the source and release record are examined. The review should preserve the item/release identifier, source/provenance evidence, disposition, date, and reviewer without exposing private question content unnecessarily.

If user-hosted public material is introduced in the future, DMCA safe-harbor/designated-agent requirements must be evaluated separately; they are not implied by this policy.

## Release gate

A content release is not "cleared" merely because a disclaimer exists. Before activation, the applicable material must have known provenance, lawful/original use, required source/license information, no confidential-exam source, accurate non-affiliation wording, resolved high-risk complaints, and documented editorial approval according to the release lifecycle.

## Evidence retention

Preserve versioned evidence such as Git history, source metadata, corpus hashes, structural QA results, editorial/SME review records, contributor agreements, and release identifiers. Never expose the private question bank merely to prove that this evidence exists.
