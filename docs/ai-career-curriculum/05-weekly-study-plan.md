# Weekly Study Plan

## Default: 12 Hours

| Workstream | Hours | Required output |
|---|---:|---|
| Theory and official documentation | 3 | Annotated concept map or decision note |
| Implementation | 5 | Tested code, benchmark or laboratory result |
| Portfolio and technical writing | 2 | README, ADR, diagram, runbook or case-study increment |
| Primary-paper reading | 1 | One-page paper card with claim, method, limits and next experiment |
| Interview and communication | 1 | Recorded explanation, mock question or leadership story |

## Alternative Loads

| Weekly load | Theory | Build | Portfolio | Paper | Interview | Adjustment |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 6 hours | 1.5 | 2.5 | 1 | 0.5 | 0.5 | Extend each roadmap month to six weeks |
| 15 hours | 3.5 | 7 | 2.5 | 1 | 1 | Add one deeper benchmark or ablation |
| 20 hours | 4 | 10 | 3 | 1.5 | 1.5 | Add open-source contribution and second implementation |

## Weekly Rhythm

1. **Plan (20 minutes):** choose one competency, one artifact and one measurable threshold.
2. **Learn:** read official/primary sources only as needed to unblock the artifact.
3. **Build:** work vertically from input to measurable output; capture traces and failures.
4. **Review (45 minutes):** demo, record evidence links, score the skill and choose the next bottleneck.
5. **Communicate:** explain the trade-off to an executive audience and to a senior engineer.

## Monthly Evaluation

- Re-score only skills with new evidence.
- Review quality, safety, latency, cost, reliability and adoption metrics.
- Conduct one architecture review and one incident/tabletop exercise.
- Remove topics that do not support a role gap or portfolio milestone.
- Require a reproducible demo and a decision document before advancing.

## Preventing Tutorial Overload

- Use one primary resource per topic and at most one supplement.
- Stop consuming when implementation can begin.
- Time-box passive learning to 25% of weekly hours.
- No second tutorial until the first produced tested code or a defensible decision.
- Every learning topic must produce a measurable artifact; notes without application do not count.

## Skill Scoring

Score using `skill-assessment.csv`: 0-1 for knowledge, 2 for guided implementation, 3 for independent delivery, 4 for architecture plus measured trade-offs, and 5 only for repeatable team/organizational adoption. Link evidence in the `evidence` column.
