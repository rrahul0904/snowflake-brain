# Leadership And Management Curriculum

## Senior Management System

| Domain | Practice | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Hiring | Role design, structured interviews, calibration and closing | Hiring plan and scorecard |
| Leveling | Observable scope, impact, autonomy and influence | Career ladder and calibration cases |
| Coaching | Diagnosis, goals, feedback and sponsorship | Development plan and follow-up record |
| Performance | Clear expectations, timely feedback and fair documentation | Performance plan template |
| Promotions | Evidence-based packet and organizational calibration | Promotion rubric |
| Delegation | Outcomes, constraints, decision rights and checkpoints | Delegation map |
| Team topology | Platform/product boundaries and cognitive load | Team interaction model |
| Senior engineers | Technical strategy, healthy dissent and leverage | Principal-engineer operating agreement |
| Roadmaps/OKRs | Outcome-based sequencing and capacity trade-offs | Quarterly roadmap and scorecard |
| Stakeholders | Decision ownership, conflict and communication cadence | Stakeholder map/RACI |
| Architecture | Principles, ADRs, review and exception process | Architecture governance charter |
| Incidents | Command, communication, recovery and learning | Incident review and action tracker |
| Technical debt | Risk/value framing and capacity allocation | Debt register and investment memo |
| Data quality | Ownership, SLOs, controls and escalation | Quality council review |
| Product partnership | Metric tree, experiment review and prioritization | Product-metric council |
| Organizational change | Sponsor network, adoption and resistance management | Change plan |
| Executive communication | Decision-first narrative, options, risk and ask | One-page memo and ten-slide deck |
| AI adoption | Portfolio governance, enablement, controls and value | AI operating model |

## Templates

### Team Charter

Mission; customers; owned outcomes; boundaries; interfaces; decision rights; operating cadence; SLOs; staffing/skills; risks; six-month measures.

### Hiring Scorecard

Role outcome; must-have evidence; structured competencies; interview questions; behavioral anchors; red flags; independent ratings; final decision rationale.

### Career Ladder

For each level define scope, complexity, autonomy, technical judgment, delivery, reliability, collaboration, leadership and organizational impact with concrete examples.

### Quarterly Roadmap

Outcome; baseline; target; initiatives; dependencies; capacity; risks; leading/lagging measures; explicit non-goals; monthly re-plan trigger.

### One-On-One

Energy; wins; blockers; decisions; feedback both directions; growth objective; relationships; workload; commitments with owners/dates.

### Incident Review

Impact; detection; timeline; contributing conditions; response; where controls failed; customer communication; corrective actions by prevention/detection/mitigation; owners and verification.

### Architecture Review

Decision; context; requirements; options; data/security/reliability/cost; failure modes; migration; observability; reversibility; recommendation; dissent and approval.

### Data-Quality Review

Critical products; owners; SLO performance; recurring failures; escaped defects; control coverage; consumer impact; remediation investment.

### Product-Metric Council

Metric definition/owner; event contract; segmentation; quality threshold; experiment use; decision history; deprecation and dispute process.

## Leadership Curriculum Outputs

Build the nine templates using portfolio evidence, run two simulated reviews, and convert at least eight career experiences into concise leadership stories. The standard is demonstrated organizational mechanism, not familiarity with management vocabulary.
