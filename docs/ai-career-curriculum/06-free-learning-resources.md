## Leadership, Communication And Delivery

- **Engineering management:** use the free [Google re:Work manager guides](https://rework.withgoogle.com/) for hiring, coaching and team effectiveness; convert each guide into an operating template rather than a course-completion claim.
- **Executive communication:** practice decision-first one-page memos, ten-slide investment proposals and architecture reviews using the templates in this curriculum.
- **Forward-deployed discovery:** use customer-development interview principles, architecture katas and the P4 case study. Evidence must include discovery notes, a value hypothesis, constraints, adoption metrics and product feedback.
